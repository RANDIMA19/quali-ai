Title: Non-numeric/malformed incident_id crash test
Student: student4 (Information Retrieval & Security Assessment)

}

Observations:
The non-numeric DataFrame index was handled successfully, and Agent 2 returned a normal anomaly-analysis result without raising an exception.

Conclusion:
No crash or unhandled error was observed for malformed/non-numeric indices.
