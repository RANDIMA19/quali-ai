Observations:
All 10 rapid failed login attempts returned HTTP 401 with the same error message. No throttling, delay, CAPTCHA, or account lockout was triggered.

Conclusion:
The endpoint allows repeated authentication attempts without effective rate limiting, increasing exposure to brute-force and credential-stuffing attacks.

Severity: High
Severity justification: No rate limiting enables automated credential-stuffing/brute-force attacks at scale.
